anti-brainrot options

The filter is on. You can tighten anything right now. Loosening (allowing more) needs the filter off first, which takes your unlock delay in the popup.

Educational videos only

Only videos in the allowed categories, or from allowed channels, can play. Everything else is paused behind a block screen. Feeds and search results still list every video; the gate is on the watch page.

Allowed categories

Always allowed channels

One per line: @handle, channel URL, channel id or the exact channel name.

Block adult sites

Redirects known adult sites to a block page. Uses a list that ships inside the extension, plus your own entries. Turn it on from the popup (Chrome asks for permission to act on all sites the first time).

Also block these sites

Never block these sites

For false positives. Allow entries win over the built-in list.

Distracting sites

Feeds and short-video surfaces beyond YouTube. In pause mode a countdown and a short intention stand between you and the site, then you get a timed pass drawn from a daily budget. In block mode they are simply off. Turn the feature on from the popup (Chrome asks for permission to act on all sites the first time).

Your own patterns

One per line. site.com blocks the whole site, site.com/ only its front page, site.com/path everything under that path.

Exceptions that stay open

Same grammar. Use it for the useful corner of a blocked site, for example reddit.com/r/programming or netflix.com/browse/my-list.

Passes

Locked hours

During these hours the filter turns itself on and cannot be turned off, not even with the countdown.

Prevent removal

Chrome gives an extension no way to protect itself, so this works in two layers. The switch in the popup sends the extensions page, where the Remove button and the on/off switch live, to a block page while the filter is on; Chrome asks for the tabs permission the first time. The toolbar menu can still remove the extension. A browser policy closes that door too: Chrome then treats Anti-Brainrot as managed and refuses to remove or turn it off until the policy file is deleted with administrator rights.

Browser policy on Linux

Run this once and restart Chrome. Chrome installs its own copy from the update manifest on the website, keeps it updated and marks it "installed by your administrator". Your settings carry over.


      
Undo: delete /etc/opt/chrome/policies/managed/anti-brainrot.json and restart Chrome, which then removes the extension. On Windows and macOS Chrome only honours a policy for an extension outside the Chrome Web Store on a managed machine (domain joined, MDM or Chrome Enterprise Core). Steps for every platform are on the website.
